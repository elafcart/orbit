## Scope of Work

### Phase 1 — Research & Information Architecture (Week 1)

- Review your analytics, Search Console, and best-selling categories to understand what people actually look for
- Define the four shopper journeys: the loyal local, the wedding/tux customer, the gift buyer, and the out-of-town visitor
- **Rebuild the category structure** — collapse 60+ duplicated categories into a clean, intuitive set of 6–8 top-level collections with a proper mega menu
- Map the browse → size → buy path and design out every unnecessary click
- Competitive review of how leading independent menswear retailers present heritage and product

### Phase 2 — Design (Weeks 2–3)

I'll design in **Figma** and share clickable mockups for your approval before I build anything. Desktop and mobile, every screen.

- **Homepage** — a working hero (properly encoded video or a strong photographic alternative), your 1926 heritage told visually, brand showcase, featured collections, seasonal/centennial edit, trust signals, reviews, and newsletter capture
- **Shop & category pages** — real category imagery, faceted filtering by size / colour / brand / price, and product cards with quick-add
- **Product page** — image gallery, a proper **size and fit guide**, clear shipping and returns visibility, reviews, intelligent related products, and stock urgency
- **Cart & checkout** — a calmer, shorter flow with a progress indicator, trust badges, and shipping clarity up front
- **About page** — a genuine heritage timeline from the 1926 mercantile store through the woolen mill to today
- **Contact & fittings page** — real address, phone, hours, embedded map, and a **tux and suit fitting enquiry form**
- **Mobile-first responsive refinement** across every template
- **Design system** — type scale, colour palette, spacing, buttons, and reusable components so future pages stay consistent

### Phase 3 — Build & Technical (Weeks 3–5)

This includes **everything in my technical audit**, at no extra cost:

- Fix the broken hero video (H.264 MP4 + WebM, fast-start, poster fallback) — verified on Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and mobile
- Replace all demo contact content with your real store details; fix the HTTP 500 on `/contact/` and redirect `/contact-2/` → `/contact/`
- Write a genuine standalone Refund & Returns policy and correct the Privacy Policy
- Security hardening — remove admin username exposure, disable XML-RPC, block author scanning, disable file editing, add login rate-limiting and security headers
- Full SEO setup — Yoast or Rank Math, meta titles and descriptions, **LocalBusiness and Product schema**, robots.txt fix, Search Console and GA4 connected
- Image pipeline — WebP conversion, compression, lazy loading, responsive `srcset`
- Performance — page caching, minification, deferred JavaScript, database cleanup
- Catalogue restructuring — convert size-as-product listings into proper WooCommerce variable products, populate missing SKUs, normalise product titles, add canonical tags and 301 redirects
- Accessibility improvements toward WCAG 2.1 AA
- Staging environment, full backups, and core/theme/plugin updates

### Phase 4 — Launch & Handover (Week 5)

- Cross-browser and cross-device QA
- Zero-downtime launch with redirect mapping so you keep every search ranking you have
- **Recorded walkthrough video** showing you how to add products, manage variations, and use the new tools
- 14 days of priority support after launch
